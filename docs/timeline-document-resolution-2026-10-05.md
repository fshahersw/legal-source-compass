It calls the existing `corpus_pdf_documents_by_id_v1` RPC in batches of at most 500, requires a matching source and ID from the active batch, and checks the returned native case ID against the entry's recorded docket relationship. Exact rows replace cached copies before hash deduplication, preserving fresh held/open status. Held rows remain unlinked and disclose no hash or byte count.
