# Time Limits release 2026-10-17.1 — consumer protection acts

Adds a thirteenth claim type, `consumer_protection` (state consumer-protection / deceptive-practices acts), to the
Time Limits calculator. Every prior rule, source and coverage cell from `2026-10-12.4` is carried forward unchanged
(builder report: 0 rejected entries, 0 discrepancies, 0 changed rules).

## Coverage

| Status | Jurisdictions |
| --- | --- |
| Date computable (23) | AK, AL, CA, CO, CT, HI, ID, IL, IN, KY, LA, MA, MI, NC, NE, OH, OR, SC, SD, TX, VA, WA, WI |
| Flagged, no date (2) | NH (three-year look-back exemption, not a limitations period), WY (written notice precedes a one-year suit period) |
| Not recorded (26) | AR, GA, TN (official code only through a gated publisher); UT (2025 text of § 13-11-19 has no limitations subsection); AZ, DC, DE, FL, IA, KS, MD, ME, MN, MO, MS, MT, ND, NJ, NM, NV, NY, OK, PA, RI, VT, WV (no act-specific provision; courts apply a general statutory-liability period, not yet mapped) |

Two-limb provisions are modelled as limbs combined by "later": Colorado § 6-1-115, South Dakota § 37-24-33 (occurrence or
discovery) and Texas § 17.565. Where a later alternative depends on facts the calculator does not collect (an Attorney
General action in Kentucky and Ohio, the last payment in Michigan), the computed date uses the stated limb and the rule's
conditions name the alternative. Alabama's four-year outer limit is recorded but not computed because of its
contract/warranty exception.

Captured text corrected two figures that differ from commonly cited summaries: South Dakota's period is four years
(SL 2008, ch 206), and Utah § 13-11-19 as amended in 2025 no longer carries a limitations subsection.
