**_Effective 5/3/2023_**  
**78B-2-307.****Within four years.**  
An action may be brought within four years:[](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html)
(1)after the last charge is made or the last payment is received: [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (a)upon a contract, obligation, or liability not founded upon an instrument in writing; [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (b)on an open store account for any goods, wares, or merchandise; or [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (c)on an open account for work, labor or services rendered, or materials furnished;
[](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html)
(2)for a claim for relief or a cause of action under the following sections of [Title 25, Chapter 6, Uniform Voidable Transactions Act](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title25/Chapter6/25-6.html?v=C25-6_2017050920170509): [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (a)Subsection [25-6-202(1)(a)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title25/Chapter6/25-6-S202.html?v=C25-6-S202_2017050920170509#25-6-202(1)(a)), except in specific situations where the time for action is limited to one year under Section [25-6-305](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title25/Chapter6/25-6-S305.html?v=C25-6-S305_2017050920170509); [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (b)Subsection [25-6-202(1)(b)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title25/Chapter6/25-6-S202.html?v=C25-6-S202_2017050920170509#25-6-202(1)(b)); or [](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html) (c)Subsection [25-6-203(1)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title25/Chapter6/25-6-S203.html?v=C25-6-S203_2017050920170509#25-6-203(1));
[](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html)
(3)for a claim involving personal property damage to the aggrieved party's motor vehicle, as defined in Section [41-6a-102](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title41/Chapter6A/41-6a-S102.html?v=C41-6a-S102_2023050320230503), or personal property from an accident involving a motor vehicle; and
[](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title78B/Chapter2/78B-2-S307.html)
(4)for relief not otherwise provided for by law.
  
  
Amended by Chapter [185](http://le.utah.gov/UtahCode/ChapterLookup.jsp?chap=185&sess=2023GS), 2023 General Session
